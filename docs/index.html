Physics and numerics

Deterministic thermal Compton scattering.

Every matrix element retains its physical meaning: the multigroup, angle-binned cross section for photon energy transfer from group \(g\) to group \(g'\) within scattering angle bin \([\xi_{i-1/2},\,\xi_{i+1/2}]\).

KPB

Scattering kernel

The Kershaw–Prasad–Beason thermal Compton kernel

The multigroup, angle-binned scattering cross section is defined as:

$$\sigma\bigl(g \to g',\,[\xi_{i-\frac{1}{2}},\,\xi_{i+\frac{1}{2}}];\,T\bigr) = \frac{2\pi \displaystyle\int_{\mathcal{E}_{g-\frac{1}{2}}}^{\mathcal{E}_{g+\frac{1}{2}}} \int_{\mathcal{E}'_{g'-\frac{1}{2}}}^{\mathcal{E}'_{g'+\frac{1}{2}}} \int_{\xi_{i-\frac{1}{2}}}^{\xi_{i+\frac{1}{2}}} \mathcal{K}\;d\xi\;d\mathcal{E}'\;d\mathcal{E}} {\mathcal{E}_{g+\frac{1}{2}} - \mathcal{E}_{g-\frac{1}{2}}}$$

where \(\mathcal{K}(\mathcal{E},\,\mathcal{E}',\,\xi;\,T)\) is the thermal Compton kernel, \(\mathcal{E}\) and \(\mathcal{E}'\) are the incoming and outgoing photon energies, \(\xi = \cos\theta\) is the cosine of the scattering angle, and \(T\) is the electron temperature.

NumPy format

Download as .npy arrays directly loadable with numpy.load(). Edge-case dimension reduction for scalar/1D/2D results.

i

Accuracy: Collapsing to coarser grids is exact when requested boundaries align with stored fine-grid boundaries. For non-aligned boundaries, the Compton kinematic formula determines output-group assignment; typical relative error on dominant matrix elements is ≤ 5%.

Temperature interpolation: Custom temperatures are interpolated linearly in log-T between the two nearest stored values (adjacent stored temperatures have a ratio of ≈1.25).

Open and reproducible

Compton scattering matrices for astrophysical transport.

MACE provides pre-computed multigroup, angle-resolved Compton scattering cross sections for radiation-hydrodynamics codes. Configure the grids you need and download ready-to-use NumPy arrays, or retrieve the source code and generation scripts.

ComptonMatrixExact

A standalone solver that evaluates the exact thermal Compton-scattering kernel (Kershaw, Prasad & Beason) for arbitrary energy grids, angle discretizations, and electron temperatures. It produces the multigroup cross-section matrices that MACE serves.

GLOW — Group-resolved Low-density Opacity Warehouse

A public library of multigroup opacities extending to low-density astrophysical plasmas. GLOW provides group-resolved opacity inputs for supernovae, tidal disruption events, accretion flows, stellar envelopes, radiative shocks, and other radiation-hydrodynamics problems.
